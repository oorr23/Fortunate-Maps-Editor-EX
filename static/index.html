Portal

Cooldown time in milliseconds. 0 means no cooldown.

Button

Timer in seconds. 0 means the button is not held after a player leaves. A negative value keeps it on after the first press.

Spawn point

Radius is in tiles (0 pins spawns to this tile). Weight controls how often this spawn is chosen.

Instructions

Draw. Pick a tile from the palette carousel, then tap or drag on the map. Dock tools (brush, line, fill, and the rest) paint many tiles at once.

Tile settings. Portals, buttons, and team spawns have extra options. Shift-click or double-click a tile; on a phone, double-tap or use Tile settings (yellow-crosshair / focused tile).

  • Portal: cooldown in milliseconds (0 = none).
  • Button: timer in seconds (0 = off when you leave; negative = stays on).
  • Spawn: radius in tiles and weight (how often it is chosen).

Spawns & Mars Balls. Mars Balls sit on the floor; a map can have at most two. Place team spawns, then set radius and weight in tile settings.

Map edits. Resize from Map. Rotate, flip, and mirror from the tools row. Cut or copy a rectangle, then use Paste and tap where it should go. On a phone, the selection must be an odd number of tiles wide and tall (1, 3, 5…) so paste can sit on the center tile. Undo/redo with the dock buttons or Z / Y.

Save & collab. Maps auto-save to this device’s cache. File → Collab copies a live share link. Put your name on the left and cursor color on the right of that row. Swipe in from the right edge of the screen for room chat (join/leave lines and messages). Esc, a swipe back, or a tap outside closes it.

Happy map making.
